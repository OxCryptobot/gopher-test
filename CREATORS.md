# GOPHER AI — creator pitch criteria (C5)

Who to pitch **after beta**. Criteria only. No fake follower counts. No vanity metrics.

Public face: **GOPHER AI**. Price: **$19/month**. Waitlist is the door. Brand: `BRAND.md`. Plan: `SHIP_AZ.md` Phase C.

## Pitch when

- Beta invite from waitlist works end-to-end (mail → hole → Telegram or Ask).
- Staging proven; production promote only per `PROMOTE.md`.
- Demo stills/reel show **real** FETCH + directory (no stock).

## Who (fit over reach)

1. **Retro / protocol web** — people who cover Gopher, finger, BBS, CRT/terminal UX, indie web directories.
2. **Indie hackers** — builders shipping small paid tools; honest waitlist + parked checkout stories land.
3. **Crypto twitter (selective)** — ticker/FETCH interest without hype accounts; prefer builders and researchers over shill threads.
4. **PWA / local-first** — device login, offline-ish hole, no fake cloud claims.
5. **Newsletter / blog** — protocol nostalgia or “AI assistant without chat sludge” angles; link waitlist, not invented SLA.

## Do

- Lead with the one-liner and three bullets (`MARKETING.md` / `LAUNCH.md`).
- Offer phosphor share card + press hole (`/press`, `press/`).
- State $19/month and that checkout is parked until billing is real.
- Point to staging only if labeled TEST; press uses production after promote.

## Don’t

- Buy engagement or invent follower counts.
- Pitch SMS/voice numbers that do not exist.
- Claim awards, ARR, download counts, or 99.9% uptime.
- Name internal vendors in public pitch copy.
