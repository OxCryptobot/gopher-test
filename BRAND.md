# GOPHER AI — brand book

This is the working book. Not a 40-page PDF. Phosphor on near-black. If a rule is not here, default to the hole.

## Color

| Token | Hex | Use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `--bg` | `#070908` | Ground. Near-black, not pure black. |
| `--bg-2` | `#0c110c` | Recessed panels. |
| `--fg` | `#39ff14` | Phosphor. Body, logo, chrome. |
| `--fg-mid` | `#2bc414` | Secondary phosphor. |
| `--fg-dim` | `#1a7a12` | Status, rules, quiet chrome. |
| `--fg-bright` | `#b8ff9a` | Lead line. Glow on the breath. |
| `--err` | `#ff6a3d` | Faults. Orange sludge, not red panic. |
| Glow | `0 0 12px rgba(57, 255, 20, 0.28)` | Phosphor bloom. Do not overdo. |

Theme color is `#070908`. No extra palettes. No gradients as decoration.

## Type

Stack, in order:

`ui-monospace, "SF Mono", "Cascadia Code", "IBM Plex Mono", Menlo, Consolas, "Courier New", monospace`

No sans. No display faces. Size follows the hole (`clamp(14px, 2.7vw, 16px)`). Letter-spacing on the wordmark only.

## Voice

Orders, not chat. Selectors, not sludge.

You send an order. It fetches. Same hole, either way.

- Imperative. Short. Numbered when it is a list.
- Product line: *Paid phone assistant. Search, then do the task.*
- Tag: *1991 protocol. 2026 interface.*
- Controls stay verbs: GO, ENTER, GOT IT. Play is a side quest, not the pitch.
- Name the product **GOPHER AI**. Always both words. Always caps.

## Do

- Phosphor `#39ff14` on `#070908`.
- Monospace. CRT scan if the surface is the hole.
- Credit **GOPHER AI**. Link the production hole.
- Print only numbers you have. Waitlist is the door. Public price is $19/month. Do not invent other prices.
- Stage on test, then promote. Never ship a half-built hole to production first.

## Don't

- Chatbot sludge. "How can I help." Soft marketing paragraphs.
- Fake metrics, download counts, ARR, awards, invented SLA, invented prices.
- Sans-serif splash pages, stock photos, extra colors.
- Substitute a cartoon for the ASCII gopher.
- Pretend cloud accounts, billing checkout, or a phone number exist. They do not yet. Price is named; checkout is not live. Telegram/Resend are parked until env.
